Values expressed as % of national GDP to allow meaningful cross-country comparison independent of country size. EU-27 figures are GDP-weighted averages from Eurostat GFS (table gov_10a_exp).

Methodology note: Figures represent consolidated general government expenditure for Spain (Estado, Comunidades Autónomas, Entidades Locales and Social Security), based on IGAE data and Eurostat COFOG classification. Intra-governmental transfers are netted out to avoid double counting. Numbers are rounded to the nearest €1 billion — consult the official sources below for exact figures.
Official Data Sources

IGAE — Intervención General de la Administración del Estado

Primary source for Spain's public expenditure statistics (COFOG classification). Publishes annual consolidated accounts of all public administrations.

igae.pap.hacienda.gob.es

Ministerio de Hacienda — Presupuestos Generales del Estado

Official State Budget documentation including detailed functional and economic classifications of central government expenditure.

hacienda.gob.es — PGE 2023

Eurostat — Government Finance Statistics

EU-harmonised data on Spain's public expenditure by COFOG function. Enables cross-country comparisons using standardised methodology.

ec.europa.eu/eurostat — GFS Database

Portal de Transparencia del Gobierno de España

Official transparency portal with budget execution data, transfers, and public investment figures broken down by ministry and policy area.

transparencia.gob.es

AIReF — Autoridad Independiente de Responsabilidad Fiscal

Independent fiscal oversight body. Publishes spending reviews and analysis of public expenditure efficiency across all government levels.

airef.es — Publicaciones

Seguridad Social — Informe Económico Financiero

Detailed Social Security budget report covering pensions, unemployment benefits, healthcare contributions and other social protection programs.

seg-social.es — Presupuestos
Data based on IGAE, Ministerio de Hacienda and Eurostat official publications.  ·  Figures are approximate and rounded.  ·  Always verify with primary sources linked above.
